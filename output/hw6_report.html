  • Safe by construction. The AI that reads vendor mail has no email or payment tools and cannot make the buy decision — a plain program does, straight from the handbook. Across 22 practice and exam documents plus two attacks of my own, no hidden instruction ever triggered a tool, and every real quote was read correctly.
  • Cheap. About $17 a month at 1,000 documents a day — the price of one hoodie — and less than the undefended version ($38/mo), because it calls the model once.
  • Keep a person in the loop. Let it draft the vendor, price, and a recommend / do-not call for the buyer to approve. The email and payment tools stay off behind a human sign-off, as they are today.
  • One caveat. A brand-new hiding trick could still slip a wrong number past the code layer; the design layer still blocks any email or payment, and the buyer’s approval is the final backstop.
